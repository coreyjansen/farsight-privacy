Farsight Auto privacy policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Farsight Auto ("the app") is an Android Auto app that shows a map, driving alerts and gauges on a car display. It is made by CoreyJansen ("we", "us").

This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • We do not run any servers for this app and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information.
  • The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.
  • Your location is used on your phone to show your position on the map and to find places near you. To do that, the app relies on services run by Google and on one third-party map tile service, described below. Those services receive some location-related information directly from your phone.

Information the app uses

Location

The app asks for precise location. It uses your location to:

  • show and follow your position on the map in the car;
  • give turn-by-turn directions when you start a trip;
  • look up addresses and places near you when you search;
  • load the parts of the alert heat map that cover the area on screen.

Google's Navigation SDK, which the app uses for the map and directions, includes a background location permission and a location service that runs while navigation is active. The app uses location only while it is running on the car display or showing its own screens on the phone.

Saved places and settings

Home, Work, recent destinations and your app settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are not sent to us. They are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer.

Alerts from the companion app

The app displays alerts, speed and status sent to it by the separate Farsight app installed on the same phone. This information is passed between the two apps on your device and is not sent to us.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows an alert on the car display when another app is in front. Notification content is created on your phone and is not sent to us.

Services that receive information from your phone

Google Navigation SDK and Google Maps

The map, routes and turn-by-turn directions are provided by Google. When you use them, your phone sends information to Google, including your location, the route and destination, and device and usage information. Google's use of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Platform terms. You are shown Google's navigation terms the first time the map is set up.

Address and place search

Search uses the address lookup service built into Android. On most phones this is provided by Google. The text you search for and the area you are searching in are sent to that service.

Alert heat map tiles

When the heat map is switched on, the app downloads map tiles for the area on screen from a third-party map tile server that we do not operate (host tiles.cmbe.cc). Each request contains the tile position (an area about 2 km wide at the usual zoom level) and standard connection details such as your IP address and the app's name and version. It does not contain your name, account or exact position. Tiles are cached on your phone. You can switch the heat map off in the app's Map settings, and no requests are then made.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or receive your location, searches, saved places or driving data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • The app does not write your saved places, coordinates or map tile positions to the device log.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect your data, we have nothing to delete. Data stored on your phone by the app (saved places, recents, settings, cached map tiles) is removed when you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Data held by Google or the tile service is handled under their own policies.

Children

The app is intended for drivers and is not directed at children.
